Frequently Asked Questions

My wearable won't turn on

Place it on the charger for at least 15 to 30 minutes, then press and hold the side button for 10 seconds.

Notifications aren't showing on my watch

Check that Bluetooth is turned on and notification permissions are enabled in the companion app.

Wearable isn't syncing with my phone

Toggle Bluetooth off and on, keep both devices close together, and reopen the companion app.

Battery drains faster than usual

Lower screen brightness, turn off Always-On Display, and check for recent firmware updates.
