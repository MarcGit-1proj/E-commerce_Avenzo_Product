Why choose Avenzo?

Avenzo makes the repair process easy from beginning to end. We can help you find the most convenient service option, give you an estimate, and keep you informed along the way. Avenzo-certified repairs are guaranteed, and our expert technicians use only genuine Avenzo parts — designed, tested, and manufactured to our safety and performance standards.

Questions? Answers.

Where can I check on an existing repair with Avenzo?

To track the status of an existing repair, sign in to My Support.

You can check your coverage status.

Where can I find a certified repair that's covered by Avenzo's Limited Warranty or Avenzo Care+?

Avenzo retail stores and our network of Avenzo Authorized Service Providers offer certified repairs using genuine Avenzo parts. Only repairs performed directly by Avenzo or by an Authorized Service Provider are covered by Avenzo's Limited Warranty and Care+ plans.

Can I repair my own device?

Self Service Repair is intended for individuals with the knowledge and experience to repair electronic devices. If you are experienced with the complexities of repairing electronic devices, Self Service Repair provides you with access to genuine Avenzo parts, tools, and repair manuals to perform your own out-of-warranty repair.
