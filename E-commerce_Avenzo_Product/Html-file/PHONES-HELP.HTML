Frequently Asked Questions

My phone won't turn on

Try holding the power button for 10 seconds, or charge for at least 30 minutes before retrying.

How do I check my warranty?

Go to Support → Customer Care → Check Warranty and enter your device's serial number.

Update keeps failing

Make sure you have at least 20% battery and a stable Wi-Fi connection, then retry.

Phone is overheating

Close background apps, remove the case while charging, and avoid direct sunlight.
