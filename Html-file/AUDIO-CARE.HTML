Frequently Asked Questions

One earbud is quieter than the other

Clean the mesh grille gently and check your balance setting in the Bluetooth audio settings.

Earbuds won't pair

Place both earbuds in the case, close the lid for 10 seconds, then reopen and try pairing again.

Case isn't charging

Check the charging cable and port for debris, and try a different power source.

How do I update the firmware?

Firmware updates install automatically when the earbuds are connected and charging near your phone.
