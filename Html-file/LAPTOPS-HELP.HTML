Frequently Asked Questions

My laptop won't turn on

Connect the charger, hold the power button for 10 to 15 seconds to reset power, and try turning it on again.

How do I check my warranty?

Go to Support → Customer Care → Check Warranty and enter your laptop's serial number.

Drivers or OS update keeps failing

Keep your laptop plugged in with a stable internet connection, then run updates again.

Laptop is running hot or loud

Use the laptop on a hard, flat surface, clear vents of dust, and close heavy background apps.
